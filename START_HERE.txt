MAX Bridge / GREEN-API React test task

1) Place this folder on Desktop, for example:
   C:\Users\<username>\Desktop\MAX_GreenAPI_React_Test

2) Open PowerShell in this folder.

3) Run:
   Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process Bypass
   .\01_run_local.ps1

4) Enter your GREEN-API MAX idInstance and apiTokenInstance in the browser.

5) After checking the project, publish it:
   .\02_publish_github.ps1

6) Later updates:
   .\03_push_updates.ps1 -Message "fix: ..."

Full instructions: README.md and docs\SUBMISSION.md
